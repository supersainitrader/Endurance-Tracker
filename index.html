Protocols Reference
💪 Muscular Endurance
3–5 sets · 12–100 reps
Sets: 3–5 sets
Reps: 12–100 (12–25 most practical)
Rest: 30–180 sec between sets
Exercises: Push-ups, pull-ups, planks, wall sits, KB swings
Train to failure: Approach or reach muscular failure
⚠ Minimize eccentric: Don't slow the lowering phase
⚠ No Olympic lifts at high reps — form degrades too fast
🏃 Long-Duration Cardio
1 continuous set · 12min–several hours
Structure: 1 continuous effort
Duration: 12 min to several hours
Intensity: Below 100% VO₂ max — steady rhythm
Benefit: Mitochondrial density + microcapillary network
💡 Wall hack: Brief acceleration when bonking — taps phosphocreatine/glycogen pathways
⚡ HIIT Anaerobic
3–12 sets · above 100% VO₂ max
3:1 ratio (low-skill): 30s work / 10s rest — assault bike, rower
1:5 ratio (high-skill): 20s work / 100s rest — squats, KB movements
Progression: Start at 3 sets. Add 1–2 per week
Goal: Explosive bursts under repeated high fatigue
❤️ HIIT Aerobic
3–12 sets · 1:1 work:rest
Target: Cardiac stroke volume (more blood per beat)
Protocol: Run 1 mile hard → rest same duration → repeat
Volume: Build to 4–7 total working miles
Ratio: Strictly 1:1 work:rest
🫁 Respiratory Warm-Up + 🧘 Cool-Down + 💧 Hydration
Warm-up: 3 min deep breathing — raise chest + belly before any bout
Cool-down: 5–20 min lying quietly with slow nasal exhales immediately post-workout
Hydration formula: body weight (lbs) ÷ 30 = oz per 15 min of exercise
⚠ Caution: Avoid heavy gulps above 70% VO₂ max unless gut-trained
